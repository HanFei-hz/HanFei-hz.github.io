Explore my research ↗

I am a PhD student in Computer Science and Technology, jointly trained by Westlake University and Zhejiang University. Advised by Prof. Dixia Fan, I study bioinspired propulsion and robot learning in Westlake University’s i4FSI Lab, with expected graduation in June 2027.

Since December 2023, I have pursued parallel research on the REEF propulsor platform and quadruped swimming, connecting force measurements, dynamics modeling, policy learning and robot experiments. My earlier work covers deep-sea soft grippers and storm-petrel locomotion.

During my April–October 2026 visit to Penn State University, I lead research on multilegged swimming with Baxi Chong’s Locomotion in Biology and Robotics (LiBR) Lab, including reduced-order dynamics modeling, numerical simulation and experimental design. The project remains ongoing in collaboration with researchers from Harvard University, Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) and other institutions; the manuscript is in preparation.

Research projects

My work links experimental systems with models and learning algorithms. The REEF platform and quadruped swimmer are parallel parts of a continuing doctoral research program.

Dec 2023 – present · Doctoral research

REEF and learning in real fluids

Contribute to the REEF platform and learning-control pipeline: synchronized experiments, force-feedback data, hydrodynamic modeling, imitation learning, offline learning and online adaptation. Related work develops HydroWrench, a dataset and benchmark for dynamic-load reconstruction.

Nature Mechanical Engineering · Under review / HydroWrench · ICLR 2027 · Under review

Dec 2023 – present · Parallel work with REEF

Quadruped swimming and robot learning

Collect experimental force data and build an LSTM hydrodynamic model for multi-objective gait optimization. Study safe reinforcement learning, leg-phase coordination, swimming behavior planning and skill distillation, connecting single-leg experiments with whole-robot validation.

Learn to Swim · ICRA 2025 / Swimming Under Constraints · ICRA 2026 / From Gaits to Routes · ICRA 2027

Applied collaboration with DEEP Robotics

Arctic expedition application · Aug 2025

The collaboration adopted my force-testing, modeling and motion-parameter-optimization framework. I participated in discussions on specialized foot design as a project contributor.
